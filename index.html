Both pucks show the same behaviour: resistance to change in motion. That resistance is called inertia.

Newton’s First Law states:

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and direction, unless acted upon by an unbalanced force.

Because it describes inertia so perfectly, it’s often called the Law of Inertia.

02 Newton's Second Law

The relationship you discovered has a name: Newton's Second Law.

F = m × a

Force = mass × acceleration

Greater force → greater acceleration. Greater mass → smaller acceleration.

03 Summary

Newton's Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Forces always come in pairs. If A pushes B, B pushes back on A with the same force.

A ⇄ B

This is why you can walk, swim, and jump — you push against the ground/water, and it pushes you forward.
